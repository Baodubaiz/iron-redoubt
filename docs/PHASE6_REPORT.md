## Not verified / known limitations

1. **No device was connected (`adb devices` was empty)**, so these are not verified yet:
   - install;
   - first launch on a phone;
   - real touch;
   - safe areas;
   - performance;
   - audio;
   - lifecycle with real Home/Back/calls.
   Next step: connect a phone with USB debugging and run `tools\install_apk.bat`, or send the APK to testers with `docs/PLAYTEST_GUIDE.md`.
2. **Not a Play Store build** (no AAB, no store listing). Testers will see "unknown app" / Play Protect prompts.
3. **Closing the app mid-match loses that match.** Campaign progress is saved between matches.
4. **Challenges use the device clock,** so they are spoofable offline (a known limitation).
5. **iOS is not built** (needs macOS + Xcode). Unchanged from Phase 1.
